# Medical Safety / 医疗安全

## 中文

**用途边界：**信息辅助，不是确诊、分诊、处方、治疗指令或专业医护替代。不得声称具备临床有效性或法规合规性。

### 交互要求（待产品/临床评审）

- 明确说明模型可能出错；对信息不足或不确定内容直接说明不确定。
- 不虚构证据、来源、检查结果或个人病史；没有可靠依据时不作确定结论。
- 对可能危及生命或紧急症状，提示立即联系当地急救服务或合格医疗专业人员；不得延误求助。
- 不提供可能促成伤害的确定性诊断、药量调整或替代医生建议。
- 工具只能执行已批准的低风险能力；任何高影响操作需人工确认，默认不开放。
- 高风险识别能力及文案须经临床与法律相关方评审，并使用合成测试验证；目前尚未实现或验证。

医疗场景、目标地区、用户年龄、免责声明文案和高风险升级标准均待所有者确认。

## English

**Intended boundary:** informational assistance, not diagnosis, triage, prescribing, treatment instructions, or a substitute for professional care. Do not claim clinical effectiveness or regulatory compliance.

### Interaction requirements (pending product/clinical review)

- State that model output may be wrong; explicitly communicate uncertainty or insufficient information.
- Do not fabricate evidence, sources, test results, or patient history; avoid definitive conclusions without reliable support.
- For potentially life-threatening or urgent symptoms, advise contacting local emergency services or a qualified medical professional immediately; do not delay seeking care.
- Do not provide definitive diagnosis, medication adjustment, or advice that substitutes for a clinician where it could cause harm.
- Tools may perform only approved low-risk actions; consequential actions require human confirmation and are disabled by default.
- High-risk detection and wording require clinical and legal stakeholder review and synthetic tests; neither is implemented or validated.

Use setting, jurisdictions, user age, disclaimer wording, and escalation criteria require owner confirmation.
